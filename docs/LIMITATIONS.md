# Limitations

- Two corrections and 19 cells are selected examples, not an accuracy benchmark.
- Seven ambiguous readings remain unresolved.
- Assignment tiers must not be merged into a claim of strict acceptance.
- Tests check integrity and consistency, not handwriting recognition.
- Full data, training, weights, strict release and generator are not included.
  Their hashes do not make the complete study reproducible.
- Technical-note statistics are reported results; they cannot all be recomputed here.
- Historical strict v21 covers 22,106 of 23,859 cells (92.6527%). Future completion
  is not assumed. Coverage, accuracy and decipherment are different quantities.
- Reproducing statistics does not establish absence of meaning, a unique historical
  algorithm or the manuscript author's motive.
- This package is not an independent replication or journal peer review.
- Third-party transcriptions retain their own authorship and applicable rights.

Do not describe this repository as the complete reproducible study.
