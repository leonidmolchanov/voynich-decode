# Limitations

- The work has not yet been independently replicated or peer-reviewed.
- 100% structural coverage does **not** equal 100% correctness, translation, or
  meaning.
- The full layer contains results from different confirmation stages; the
  provenance of each row is preserved in `assignment_tier` and
  `assignment_source`.
- The current authority is strict-v82 (23,859/23,859 = 100% structural
  coverage; the lock + release/post-publish + independent completion audit are
  included). "100%" means only a structural outcome per cell (an anonymous
  machine factor, or an explicit abstention) — **not** translation, reading of a
  language, or established meaning: the language remains UNKNOWN, and
  "hoax vs. a very verbose cipher" is indistinguishable from the text alone.
- The historical step strict-v21 (≈92.65%) is included separately as a previous
  step in the lineage and must not be conflated with the current 100% authority
  (v82).
- Seven physically disputed signs are left as unforced surface readings — the
  ambiguity is recorded, not overwritten. In strict-v82 every cell (those seven
  included) still carries a concrete factor; abstention is an allowed outcome of
  the procedure that is simply not exercised in this release (0 abstentions).
- The generator reproduces the main diagnostic properties but over-produces the
  number of distinct forms and does not reproduce vocabulary concentration
  accurately enough.
- Long-range topical clustering is compatible with both a meaningful text and
  local memory/drift; on its own it does not settle the dispute.
- A complex, verbose homophonic cipher remains a possible competitor.
- Commercial, prestige, esoteric, or didactic motives are interpretations, not
  established historical facts.
- The illustrations may carry independent information and need not be a
  line-by-line commentary on the text.
