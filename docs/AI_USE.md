# Use of AI and the role of the researcher

Language models and machine learning were used in this project to accelerate
programming, anomaly search, cross-checking of reports, ranking of disputed
cells, model sweeps, and preparation of the reader-facing narrative.

AI is **not** treated as the author of the decipherment or as an independent
source of scientific conclusions. The human decided:

- the research unit and the denominator;
- the hypotheses and the order in which they were tested;
- the physical train/test splits;
- the negative and permutation controls;
- the strict, quarantine, and abstain criteria;
- the admissible and inadmissible ways of phrasing the conclusion;
- the decisions to discard attractive but unstable correlations.

Reproducibility does not depend on trusting any particular LLM. The public
results must be verifiable from the data, the code, the frozen splits, and the
saved outputs. AI here is an amplifier of the speed and scale of analysis; the
framing of the problem, the cost of error, and the responsibility for the
conclusion remain with the operator.
